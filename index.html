How to survive

Keep your ❤ health up by never letting 🍖 food or 💧 water run dry. ⚡ Stamina drains while you flap and refills while gliding, perching or on the ground.

Flying

A / FLAP (hold) to climb and speed up. Release to glide. Stick steers. RT / DIVE to dive fast, LT to brake. B / LAND perches on the nearest tree, rock or your nest. Right stick or a drag on the right side moves the camera.

Food & water

Dive onto mice, rabbits and songbirds to snatch them, then press X / ACT to eat. Fly low over prey from high up: they spot you if you hover low. Scavenge carcasses, berries and grubs (crows). Land at any water's edge and press X to drink. Eagles grab fish right out of the lake.

Building (v5)

Walk or skim low over glinting materials to grab them automatically (📡 LS-click / D-pad ↓ / F scans 80 m). Y / B key / 🔨 opens the build menu: pick a structure, move to a spot (green ghost = OK) and press X / E / ACT to place; add stages later from the Here tab. View / I / 🎒 opens your bag. Landing at a nest stores your bag in the stash, which you can build from within 60 m.

Your nest

Pick up sticks under trees (and moss by rocks and logs). Perch on a tree and press X to build a nest. Landing at the nest drops off what you carry. At the nest you can sleep, save and build upgrades, up to a full tree base with walls and a roof.

Danger

Foxes hunt by day and wolves by night if you stay on the ground. Storms bring wind, cold and lightning. Shelter in a tree or your nest, and don't fly high. Y uses your bird's skill.
